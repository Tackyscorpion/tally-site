The coach that calls you.

Write down the few things that matter today. At the time you choose, your coach rings, asks how each one went, and marks them for you. Keep every non-negotiable and the streak grows. Miss one and it says so.

How it works

  • Plan. Add tasks for the day, or make them repeat on the days you pick. Repeating tasks are your non-negotiables.
  • Get the call. On your call days, at your time, the phone rings. Pick up from the lock screen. The coach asks how you are, then goes through your tasks one by one.
  • Keep the streak. A day counts when every non-negotiable is done. Freezes exist for illness and travel, granted by the coach.
  • Text when you can’t talk. Message the coach from the app or reply to a missed call from the lock screen.
