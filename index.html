How it works

No reminders to swipe away. A real call, at your time, about your actual day.

1

Plan the day

Add today’s tasks, or set them to repeat on the days you pick. Repeating tasks are your non-negotiables.

2

Pick up the call

On your call days, at your time, the phone rings like any other call. Answer from the lock screen.

3

Say how it went

The coach goes through your tasks one by one and marks them. Add tomorrow’s plan while you’re talking.

4

Keep the streak

A day counts when every non-negotiable is done. Miss one and the coach says so. Ill or travelling? It can freeze a day.

Built for people who already know what to do.

Tally doesn’t teach habits. It checks whether you did them, out loud, every day you asked it to.

Up to ten non-negotiables a day

Wake-up time, training, food, sleep, the work that matters. Each one is a yes or a no.

Text when you can’t talk

Message the coach from the app, or reply to a missed call from the lock screen. It marks tasks either way.

Your voice, your name for it

Choose the coach’s voice and what to call it. Gentle, Normal or Relentless when you miss a call.

Private by design

Calls are recorded so the coach remembers. Audio is deleted after 30 days, and you can delete everything from the app.
