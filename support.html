Common questions

The coach didn’t call me

  • Check Profile → Calls: calls must be on, with at least one call day and a call time, and you must have allowed call recording.
  • Check your phone allows Tally notifications (Settings → Notifications → Tally). The call arrives as an incoming call even when the phone is locked.
  • Focus modes and Do Not Disturb can silence it like any other call.
  • If you missed the call, the coach retries according to your missed-call policy (Gentle, Normal or Relentless) in Profile.

I want the call at a different time or on different days

Profile → Calls. Changes apply from the next call. On a call you can also just tell the coach.

How do I delete my recordings?

Profile → Delete recordings. Audio is removed immediately and transcripts within 24 hours. Summaries and the coach’s note are kept so your history still makes sense. Audio is in any case deleted 30 days after each call.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Tally. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep calls until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account?

Profile → Delete account removes your account and all your data. If you can’t sign in, email us from the address on your account and we’ll do it for you.

What does the coach know about me?

Only what you put in Tally: your tasks, your results, your call settings, and what you told it on earlier calls or in chat. See the Privacy Policy for the full list and who processes it.
