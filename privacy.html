Privacy Policy

Effective 16 November 2026. Applies to the Tally iOS app and the services behind it.

Tally is a task tracker with an AI coach that calls you. To do that it stores your tasks, your call settings, and recordings and transcripts of your coaching calls. This page says exactly what is kept, where, for how long, and how to delete it.

Who is responsible

Tally is developed and operated by the Tally team (“we”). Contact details are on the Support page. For the purposes of UK and EU data protection law, we are the data controller for the data described here.

Recording your calls

Calls with your coach are recorded and transcribed. You give this consent in the app before any call can happen, and you can withdraw it at any time by turning calls off or deleting your account. The coach does not announce the recording on each call because you have already agreed to it in the app.

Audio recordings are deleted automatically 30 days after the call. Transcripts and summaries are kept so the coach can refer back to them, until you delete them (Profile → Delete recordings) or delete your account.

How long we keep things

  • Call audio: 30 days, then deleted.
  • Transcripts, summaries and the coach’s note: until you delete recordings in the app, or delete your account.
  • Tasks, streaks, settings and chat: for as long as your account exists.
  • Subscription records: for as long as your account exists, and afterwards only what accounting law requires.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete recordings in Profile removes all audio now and transcripts within 24 hours; summaries and the coach’s note are kept so your history still makes sense.
  • Turn calls off in Profile at any time. No new recordings are made.
  • Delete your account from Profile (or by contacting us) removes your account and all the data above.
  • If you are in the UK or the EU you also have the right to access, correct, export or object to the processing of your data, and to complain to your data protection authority. Contact us via the Support page and we will respond within one month.

Legal bases (UK and EU)

We process your data to perform our contract with you (running the app and the calls you subscribed to), with your consent for call recording and transcription, and on our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and working.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections and is stored with per-user access controls: every record is tied to your account and can only be read with your own sign-in. Server secrets never leave our servers; the app itself carries no private keys.

Children

Tally is not intended for children under 13 (or the higher age your country sets for consenting to online services). We do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect. The effective date at the top always shows the current version.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: see the Support page.
